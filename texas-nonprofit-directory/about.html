About the data

This directory lists every organization with a Texas address in the IRS Exempt Organizations Business Master File (EO BMF) — the IRS's official register of tax-exempt organizations. It includes 501(c)(3) public charities and private foundations, churches that have applied for recognition, social welfare groups, trade associations, social clubs, fraternal and veterans' organizations.

What each profile shows

  • Identity — legal name, EIN, mailing address and "in care of" contact.
  • Status — exemption type, public charity vs. private foundation, whether donations are deductible, and the year the IRS granted exemption.
  • Cause — the National Taxonomy of Exempt Entities (NTEE) code assigned by the IRS.
  • Finances — revenue, income and assets reported on the most recent Form 990 the IRS has processed.

Limitations

  • Organizations appear at their mailing address, which may differ from where they work.
  • Churches and small organizations are not required to file Form 990, so many show no financial data.
  • Organizations whose exemption was automatically revoked are removed from the EO BMF and won't appear here.
  • The IRS refreshes the file monthly; check the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search for the latest status.

Updating the data

Run node scripts/build-data.mjs to download the latest Texas extract (eo_tx.csv) from irs.gov and regenerate the search index. The included GitHub Actions workflow does this automatically on every deploy and once a month.
